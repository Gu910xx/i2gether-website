Who We Are

Our Homes.

Their Future.

More than accommodation — a pathway to independence.


Independent Together provides safe, supported accommodation and personalised guidance for young people across Greater Manchester, helping them build the confidence, skills, and stability needed for adult life.



Our Services
What We Offer

Built Around Every Young Person

01 — Homes

Supported Accommodation

Safe, well-managed homes designed to give young people stability, structure, and a foundation to grow.

02 — Skills

Life Skills Development

We help young people build essential skills — cooking, budgeting, and daily routines for independent living.

03 — Support

Personalised Support

Dedicated support workers provide consistent guidance, mentoring, and emotional support every step of the way.

04 — Future

Pathway to Independence

We support young people into education, employment, and confident independent living.

05 — Community

Safe, Welcoming Homes

Every property is carefully chosen and managed to feel like a real home — not a facility.
